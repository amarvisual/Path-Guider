Refund & Cancellation Policy

1. Digital Products

Path Guider delivers instantly generated digital PDF reports. Due to the nature of digital goods, we generally do not offer refunds once the PDF has been generated and delivered.

2. Exceptional Circumstances

If you experience a technical error where you are charged but do not receive your report, please contact our support team immediately for a manual delivery or a full refund.

3. Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
