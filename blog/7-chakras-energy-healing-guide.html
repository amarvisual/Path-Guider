The 7 Chakras: How to Diagnose Blocked Energy & Restore Vitality

✍️ By Ayurvedic Practitioner & Energy Healer ⏱️ 9 Min Read 📅 Updated August 2026

⚡ The 7 Energy Vortexes at a Glance:

  • Muladhara (Root): Red · Base of spine · Survival, financial security, grounding.
  • Svadhisthana (Sacral): Orange · Pelvic region · Creativity, sexual vitality, pleasure.
  • Manipura (Solar Plexus): Yellow · Upper abdomen · Willpower, confidence, digestive fire.
  • Anahata (Heart): Green · Center of chest · Compassion, forgiveness, emotional intimacy.
  • Vishuddha (Throat): Blue · Throat · Speaking truth, authentic communication.
  • Ajna (Third Eye): Indigo · Brow center · Intuition, mental clarity, spiritual vision.
  • Sahasrara (Crown): Violet / White · Top of head · Cosmic oneness, higher consciousness.

In ancient Vedic physiology, your physical body is surrounded and sustained by a subtle energetic field governed by seven primary energy vortexes known as Chakras (meaning "spinning wheels of prana").

When trauma, chronic stress, or negative emotional habits accumulate, these vortexes become sluggish or blocked, manifesting as physical exhaustion, chronic anxiety, brain fog, and creative paralysis.

1. Root Chakra (Muladhara) — Grounding & Financial Security

🔴 Muladhara — Seed Mantra: LAM

Signs of Blockage: Chronic fear about money, feeling unsafe in the world, hoarding, lower back pain, restless leg syndrome.

🌿 Healing Action: Walk barefoot on natural grass (earthing), eat root vegetables (carrots, beets), carry Red Jasper / Black Tourmaline, and practice Mountain Pose (Tadasana).

2. Solar Plexus Chakra (Manipura) — Willpower & Personal Power

🟡 Manipura — Seed Mantra: RAM

Signs of Blockage: People-pleasing, inability to make firm decisions, low self-esteem, digestive disorders, acid reflux.

🌿 Healing Action: Sun salutations (Surya Namaskar) in morning sunlight, drink ginger tea, carry Citrine or Tiger's Eye, and practice Plank / Boat Pose (Navasana).

3. Heart Chakra (Anahata) — Love, Grief & Forgiveness

🟢 Anahata — Seed Mantra: YAM

Signs of Blockage: Inability to forgive past betrayals, feeling emotionally numb or defensive, chronic heart palpitations, tight chest breathing.

🌿 Healing Action: Forgiveness journaling, deep heart-opening stretches (Camel Pose / Ustrasana), carrying Rose Quartz or Green Aventurine, and spending time in lush forests.

4. Third Eye Chakra (Ajna) — Intuition & Mental Clarity

🟣 Ajna — Seed Mantra: OM / AUM

Signs of Blockage: Brain fog, cynical skepticism, lack of spiritual inspiration, chronic migraines, sleep disturbances.

🌿 Healing Action: Silent meditation gazing at the space between brows (Trataka candle meditation), carry Amethyst, and practice Child's Pose (Balasana).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions About Chakras

How long does it take to unblock a Chakra?
With dedicated daily practice (breathwork, seed mantra chanting, and physical yoga asanas), subtle shifts can be felt within 3 to 7 days, with deep emotional integration taking 21 to 40 days.
Can a Chakra be overactive as well as underactive?
Yes. An overactive Solar Plexus creates arrogance and aggressive control, while an overactive Throat Chakra creates nonstop talking and inability to listen. Balance is the key.
